# Literature Map

- Kolda & Bader 2009: tensor decompositions and notation baseline.
- De Lathauwer et al. 2000: multilinear SVD / Tucker foundations.
- Vannieuwenhoven et al. 2012: randomized and efficient tensor approximation context.
- Minster, Saibaba, Kilmer 2020: randomized tensor algorithms and sketching relevance.
- Iannacito et al. 2026: recent sparse randomized Tucker context and comparison target.
- Minster, Li, Ballard 2024: communication and randomized tensor decomposition context.
- Amestoy/Mary et al. 2026: low precision / mixed precision numerical motivation.
- Anzt et al. 2019: mixed precision and precision-access tradeoff background.
- Smith & Karypis 2015: SPLATT and sparse tensor performance baseline.
- Li, Sun, Vuduc 2018: sparse tensor kernel performance and memory traffic motivation.
- Kaya & Ucar 2016: sparse tensor decomposition performance context.
- Li et al. 2019 PASTA: sparse tensor algebra system and benchmark context.
